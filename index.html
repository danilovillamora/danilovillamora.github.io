Selected work

Three examples of how I turn messy tasks into clear, organised systems.

Employee work schedule in Google Calendar

The problemShifts and breaks are hard to follow and hard to change when priorities move.

What I didBuilt a month-long schedule in Google Calendar with set daily blocks, a short midday break, and weekends marked OFF.

The resultEveryone can see who is working and when, and which days are off. Breaks are planned in so rest time and coverage don’t clash. Shifts are easy to update with no confusion.

Customer escalation board in Asana

The problemPhone and email escalations pile up with no clear owner, priority or status.

What I didSet up an Asana board with five stages: New, In Progress, Waiting on Customer, Escalated and Resolved. Each case is a task tagged by channel, priority and issue type.

The resultEvery case has a stage and a priority, so urgent ones stand out and resolved ones are easy to confirm.

Organised Gmail inbox

The problemUrgent emails and follow-ups get lost in one long list.

What I didKept my own Gmail at inbox zero and sorted it into Urgent, To Review and For Follow-up.

The resultNothing waits unseen. Urgent emails are separated from the rest, follow-ups are tracked, and it is always clear what needs action next.
